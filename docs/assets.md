# Asset provenance

The three-circle TPC mark, decorative shapes, icons and layouts are code-based. Interface icons use [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/) under its ISC licence. Brand app icons are generated from the original three-circle geometry.

Illustrative photographs were downloaded from Unsplash image CDN URLs on 19 October 2026 and are served from `frontend/public/images`. They illustrate atmosphere and are not photographs of the sample venues. Review the [Unsplash licence](https://unsplash.com/license) and confirm final imagery with real venue owners before public launch.
